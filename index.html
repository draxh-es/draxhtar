In the age of the great frameworks, when JavaScript ruled the land and CSS was still feared by many, a young wanderer picked up a keyboard and swore an oath: to build things that feel alive.

Since then I have forged web apps, interfaces and tiny games in the fires of late-night commits. I care about crisp code, honest design, and the little details that make people smile — a hover that bounces, a sound that clicks, a dragon that shouldn't be there.

When I'm not slaying bugs, I draw pixel art, explore old games for inspiration, and look for new quests to join.
